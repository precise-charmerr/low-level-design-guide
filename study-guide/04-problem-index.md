## How to practice a problem

1. Read only the statement first.
2. Write requirements and assumptions.
3. Identify entities and responsibilities.
4. Draw the class diagram.
5. Identify one or two justified patterns.
6. Implement the happy path.
7. Add edge cases.
8. Explain one likely future change and how your design absorbs it.
9. Compare your design with the supplied solution.
10. Refactor only after behavior works.
